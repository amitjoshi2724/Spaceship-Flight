INSTRUCTIONS

“Hold to move the spaceship. Tap to fire. Use the buttons on the lower left to rotate the spaceship. Have fun!”

📱 Touch / Mobile

  • Buttons (Bottom Left): Rotate ship
  • Hold Screen / Thrust Button: Accelerate forward
  • Tap Screen / Fire Button: Fire plasma cannons
  • Top Right (| |): Pause / Resume

💻 Keyboard & Mouse

  • Left / Right or A / D: Rotate ship
  • Up Arrow or W: Thrust engine
  • Spacebar or Left Click: Fire laser
  • P or Escape: Pause game
💡 Mission: Destroy asteroids before they hit your ship. Asteroids wrap around screen edges!
