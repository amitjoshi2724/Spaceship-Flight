❓ HOW TO PLAY ASTEROIDLE

1

Reconnaissance Clip

Watch the 1-second preview clip to analyze the asteroid's velocity vector, trajectory angle, and rotational drift across space.

2

Test-Fire Calibrated Bullets

Each challenge features a cannon velocity tier (Tiers 1 to 5). Click Test Fire (or press T) to fire tracer rounds and visually gauge bullet travel time.

3

Calculate Ballistic Lead

Your spaceship is in a fixed position. Click/drag on the radar arena or use the arrow keys to angle the cannon ahead of where the asteroid will arrive.

4

Launch & Precision Scoring

Press FIRE CANNON (or Space). A direct hit awards 100 points! Near-misses are scored based on normalized surface proximity.

5

Daily 5-Asteroid Challenge

Every day presents 5 unique asteroids. Achieve up to 500 total points, maintain your streak, and share your score card!
