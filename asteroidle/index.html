❓ How to Play Asteroidle

1
Observe the 1-Second Recon Clip: Each round, an asteroid travels across deep space for exactly 1.0 second. Watch its speed, trajectory, and rotation. Press [R] to replay the clip as often as needed.
2
Calculate the Ballistic Lead: Use the on-screen < and > loop buttons, arrow keys, or fine-tuning [-5°] [-1°] [+1°] [+5°] steppers to angle your ship where the asteroid will be when the bullet arrives.
3
Test Fire Tracers: Press [T] or tap TRACER to test-fire a non-scoring tracer bullet to gauge laser transit flight time.
4
Fire Cannon: Tap the big red FIRE button or press [SPACE] to fire your interception shot. The asteroid resumes motion from its 1.0s mark in lockstep with your bullet.
5
Continuous Proximity Scoring:
  • 🎯 Direct Hit: 100 PTS
  • ⚡ Near-Miss: Fair proximity points based on pixel distance to asteroid surface (close grazes earn 75–95+ PTS).
  • ❌ Distant Whiff: 0 PTS
5 rounds per mission. Total score is out of 500!
